@nolootid — Autoloot Blacklist

Inventory Management  All Players

Creates a personal blacklist of up to 10 items that will never be auto-picked up, regardless of your current @autoloot, @alootid, or @autoloottype settings. Works as the direct opposite of @alootid.

@nolootid +<item name or ID> Add item to blacklist
@nolootid -<item name or ID> Remove from blacklist
@nolootid reset Clear entire blacklist
@nolootid (no args) View both lists
Priority rule: The blacklist overrides everything — even @autoloot 100 and items also present in your @alootid whitelist. Items in your nolootid list stay on the ground unconditionally.
  • Typing @nolootid alone shows both lists: blacklisted items tagged [BLOCK] and whitelisted items tagged [PICK]
  • Maximum 10 items on the blacklist at once
  • List resets on logout (server defaults may repopulate it on login)

@hold — Movement Lock

Gameplay Utility  All Players

Toggles a full movement lock on your character. While active, your character cannot walk or be moved by skills. All skills fire from your current position, provided the target is within range.

@hold Toggle on / off
  • Your character is completely locked to its current cell
  • Skills still cast normally as long as the target is in range
  • Skills requiring movement to reach target will simply fail — your character does not take a single step
  • Use @hold again to deactivate
Best for: Wizards holding a Magnetic Earth position, Snipers in a fixed fire lane, Priests stationary healing in WoE, or any class that needs to cast without accidentally moving.

@raredrophide — Drop Privacy

Broadcasts  All Players

Toggles whether your character name appears in the global rare drop broadcast when you obtain a rare item. When active, the announcement shows Player instead of your name.

Adventurer's Agency — Party Recruitment Board

Social System  All Players

A built-in party recruitment system available directly from the game interface. Party leaders can advertise their group to the entire server, and solo players can browse and apply to open parties without spamming chat.

For Party Leaders

  1. 1Create a party and be the leader.
  2. 2Open the Adventurer's Agency window.
  3. 3Click the settings icon (top-right) to register.
  4. 4Fill in level range, roles needed, purpose, and note.
  5. 5Submit — listing appears instantly.

For Players Seeking a Party

  1. 1Open the Adventurer's Agency window.
  2. 2Filter by level range, role, or purpose.
  3. 3Find a listing and click Request to Join.
  4. 4Leader accepts → you receive a party invite automatically.

Listing Fields

FieldDescriptionOptions
Level RangeMin and max base level you are recruiting1 – 99
Roles NeededWhich roles are still open in your partyTanker · Healer · Dealer · Assist
PurposeWhat the party is forField · Dungeon · Quest · Paradise · Other

Rodex Restricted to Cities

Economy Protection  Restriction

The in-game mail system (Rodex) can only be opened while you are in a city or town map. Attempting to open your mailbox in a field, dungeon, or non-city area displays an error and blocks access.

Before

Rodex could be opened anywhere — including mid-dungeon or during boss fights, allowing instant item transfers on the fly.

After

Rodex is a city-only convenience feature. Cities and towns (Prontera, Geffen, Payon, etc.) are always valid locations.

MVP Persistent Respawn

Boss System  Auto

MVP bosses now survive server restarts and map reloads with their current HP intact.

Before

Any server restart silently despawned active MVPs, resetting their timers. A freshly spawned MVP could disappear without warning.

After

On restart, all live MVPs are saved to the database (map, coordinates, current HP, timer). On the next startup they are restored exactly as they were.

A restored MVP is given a 2-hour hard despawn timer from the moment it comes back, as a safety net against stale database entries.

Traps Do Not Affect Allies

PvP / WoE  Bug Fix

Traps, Fire Pillars, and other placed skill units no longer trigger on party members or guild members of the caster, in both PvE and PvP maps.

Before

With gvg_traps_target_all active, traps triggered on everyone including allies in PvP/WoE, making coordinated trap play hazardous to your own team.

After

Traps always ignore party members and guild members of the caster. Enemy players and enemy guild members remain valid targets.

Covers: Ankle Snare · Blast Mine · Claymore Trap · Land Mine · Fire Pillar · and all other placed skill units.

⚡

Trap Limit — 12 Per Skill Type

Balance  Anti-Abuse

Each player has a maximum of 12 active units per skill type on the map simultaneously.

Example: A Hunter can have 12 active Ankle Snares, 12 active Blast Mines, and 12 active Land Mines at the same time. Placing a 13th Ankle Snare automatically removes the oldest one (FIFO). Each skill type counts independently.
Problem

A single player could flood a map or chokepoint with an unreasonable number of traps, making some areas impassable.

Solution

Tactical trap play remains fully viable. No limit to how often you place traps — only how many remain active simultaneously.

🔒

Snap Cannot Escape Immobilization

Anti-Exploit  Fixed

The Monk / Champion skill Body Relocation (Snap) is now blocked when the caster is under any immobilizing status effect.

🚫
Ankle SnareSnap fails while trapped
🚫
Spider WebSnap fails while webbed
🚫
Close ConfineSnap fails while confined
🚫
Electric ShockerSnap fails while shocked
🚫
Any movement-preventing statusSnap fails if character cannot move
Why: Snap-escaping traps was a well-known exploit — a Hunter places an Ankle Snare, a Monk/Champion walks in, and instantly teleports out. This change makes immobilizing skills reliable against all classes, as originally intended.
⚔️

Sonic Blow Auto-Cast Fixed

Assassin Cross  Bug Fix

When Sonic Blow is triggered by a card effect (such as Injustice Card), the character now resumes normal attacking immediately after the skill animation ends.

Before

Sonic Blow triggered by a weapon card caused the character to stop auto-attacking entirely. The player had to manually click the enemy again to continue.

After

The character seamlessly resumes auto-attacking after the Sonic Blow animation, exactly as expected for card auto-cast builds.

🏃

Monsters Chase More Reliably

Mob AI  Two Fixes

Two separate bugs caused monsters to abandon their target mid-chase. Both have been corrected, making mob-training and AoE farming work as intended.

FixProblemResult
Chase range correction Mobs abandoned target mid-walk even when the player was well within chase range Mobs now chase reliably up to 50 cells
Obstacle path fix A 14-cell path limit meant any route around a corner or obstacle caused the mob to give up immediately Mobs now ignore the 14-cell obstacle limit and follow through any winding path
Impact: The most noticeable benefit is for AoE classes — Wizards, Hunters, Professors — who gather mobs before using a big damage skill. Previously mobs peeled off before you could group them. Mob behavior now matches classic Ragnarok Online servers.

Note: Monsters still return home once they lose their target normally. No monster was made faster or given more HP.

Favorite Tab — Full Item Protection

Inventory Safety  4 Protection Vectors

Items placed in the Favorite tab of your inventory are now completely protected from accidental loss. Once favorited, nothing can remove an item without you manually un-favoriting it first.

What Is Blocked

🚫
Selling to NPCsFavorited items do not appear in the NPC sell dialog selection.
🚫
Weapon Refining (Skill)Favorited weapons are hidden from the Whitesmith's Weapon Refine skill list. They simply don't appear as candidates.
🚫
Weapon Refining (Server Check)Even if the client sends a refine packet, the server rejects it for favorited items as a safety layer.
🚫
Production SkillsFavorited items are never consumed as materials by Pharmacy, Cooking, Rune Mastery, or any other production skill. If all stacks of a required material are favorited, the production fails instead of consuming them.
Before

The Favorite tab was only a visual organizer. Items inside could still be sold, refined, or consumed as crafting materials.

After

Favorite = truly safe. Right-click any item and select "Favorite" to protect it. Un-favorite to make it available again.

All item descriptions have been audited against the server database and corrected where they were incomplete or inaccurate. Three categories were updated across thousands of items.

CategoryWhat Was Fixed

Overweight Regen Threshold — 70%

Quality of Life  Config Change

Natural HP and SP regeneration now stops only when you are carrying more than 70% of your maximum weight, up from the previous 50%.

Old threshold50%
New threshold70%
90% limitUnchanged (no item pickup)

The 70% threshold gives significantly more room to carry drops and supplies before regen is affected. All other weight mechanics (max carry, 90% item-pickup block) remain unchanged.

Pet Auto-Feed — All Pets

Pet System  Bug Fix

The pet auto-feed toggle is now active and visible for every tameable pet in the game.

Before

The "Auto Feed" toggle was invisible in the pet interface for every pet, even though the feature was enabled on the server. Players had to manually feed pets or risk losing intimacy.

After

The toggle appears and works as intended. When enabled, the server automatically feeds your pet when its hunger drops below threshold — no manual action required.

Pets still eat the same food and grow intimacy the same way. The server just handles the timing automatically. At very low intimacy, a pet can still flee — auto-feed prevents that from happening passively.
